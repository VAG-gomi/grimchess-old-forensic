# Investigation Status

## Established

Four distinct older APK hashes are available and preserved as OLD-APK-001 through OLD-APK-004. Their package metadata, sizes, hashes, and signer certificate digests are recorded. A recoverable GrimChess source/build project is available, including engine integration, Stockfish assets, UI, simulator/paradox code, and Android Gradle inputs.

## Not established

The chronological order of the APKs is not established from filenames or filesystem timestamps. The exact source commit and build transaction that produced each APK are not established. The relationship between the recovered source snapshot and each APK is therefore bounded as recovered-source evidence, not exact production provenance.

## Not performed

No Old-versus-Current comparison is performed here. No source repair, APK replacement, re-signing, optimization, or rebuild is performed.
